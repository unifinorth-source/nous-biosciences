# NOUS BIOSCIENCES — Educational Video Production Scripts

These are narration and shot-list drafts, **not completed videos**. Target: 45–65 seconds per product. Scientific review required before publishing. Use verified compound identity, no human dosing, no clinical promises. Use 3D molecular/biological motion graphics, professional voiceover, closed captions, and a branded NOUS intro/outro.

## 01. BPC-157 (NB-001)

**Opening (0–8 s):** Cinematic NOUS logo reveal and labeled research container. Narration: “What is BPC-157, and why are researchers interested in it?”

**Context (8–23 s):** Moving scientific animation illustrating the general field of study. Narration: “BPC-157 is investigated because researchers want to understand how injured tissues coordinate healing.”

**Mechanism and evidence (23–45 s):** Animate a clearly labeled, conceptual cellular pathway. Narration: “Animal and laboratory studies have explored connective tissue, inflammation and digestive tissue responses. These findings do not establish faster recovery or pain relief in humans.”

**Limitations (45–55 s):** On-screen text: “Research context only. Product identity and batch testing must be verified.” Narration: “These scientific findings should not be mistaken for proof of benefit or safety for this specific product.”
